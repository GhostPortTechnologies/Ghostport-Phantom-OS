1 What Is WiFi Sensing?

Your ISP router can use WiFi signals to detect your physical presence, movement, and even breathing patterns inside your home. Products like Xfinity WiFi Motion, eero Motion, Linksys Aware, and Plume Motion already ship this technology. IEEE 802.11bf is standardizing WiFi sensing as a built-in capability for future routers and access points. The only way to fully eliminate this surveillance vector is to remove the ISP's WiFi radio from your home entirely.

Your ISP router is a sensor inside your home that you do not control. Disabling WiFi in software may not prevent the radio from operating in passive sensing mode.

3 Fiber Users

If you have fiber internet (Verizon FiOS, AT&T Fiber, Google Fiber), your ISP provides an ONT (Optical Network Terminal). ONTs are typically passive converters with no WiFi radio — your main concern is the separate router/gateway they provide. Ask your ISP if you can use the ONT in bridge/passthrough mode and connect GhostPort directly.

4 If You Can't Replace Your Router

If you're stuck with an ISP gateway, disable its WiFi radios. Per-ISP instructions below:

Cox Panoramic Gateway
  1. Open browser to 192.168.0.1
  2. Login (default: admin/password on sticker)
  3. Go to WiFi → WiFi Settings
  4. Disable both 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands
  5. Or call Cox and request "Bridge Mode"
Xfinity xFi Gateway
  1. Open Xfinity app or go to 10.0.0.1
  2. Navigate to WiFi settings
  3. Disable WiFi on both bands
  4. Better: Call Comcast and request "Bridge Mode" — this fully disables routing and WiFi
AT&T Gateway
  1. Open browser to 192.168.1.254
  2. Login with credentials on device label
  3. Go to Home Network → WiFi
  4. Disable both bands
  5. For passthrough: Firewall → IP Passthrough → enable
Spectrum Router
  1. Open browser to 192.168.1.1
  2. Login (default on sticker)
  3. Disable WiFi
  4. Or return the router to Spectrum (they charge $5/month rental) and buy your own modem
Verizon FiOS Gateway
  1. Open browser to 192.168.1.1
  2. Login (admin / password on sticker)
  3. WiFi → disable both bands
  4. Note: FiOS may require their router for TV service
5 Verify It's Off

After disabling your ISP router's WiFi, use GhostPort's WiFi Sensing Check (Dashboard → Diagnostics) to verify no ISP networks are still broadcasting from your home.
