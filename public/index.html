⚠ ENGAGE DREADNOUGHT?
Dreadnought Mode disables encrypted DNS (DoH) and queries root servers directly. Cloudflare is cut out as a middleman, but:

• Your ISP can see every domain you look up.
• DNS travels in plaintext on the wire.
• First queries may be slower until unbound's cache warms.

Use this when you want to distribute DNS trust across the root/TLD system instead of trusting one provider. Not recommended on hostile networks.
